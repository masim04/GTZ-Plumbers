Our Story

Dedicated to Safe, Clean, Code-Perfect Systems

Founded in Orange, CA, GTZ Plumbing Service was built on a simple promise: to eliminate the headaches, missed appointments, and poor craftsmanship common in the home service industry. We wanted to offer homeowners and commercial businesses plumbing solutions they could fully trust.

Over the past 15+ years, we have grown from a single mobile service truck into a fully equipped team capable of handling large-scale repiping, code-compliant gas installations, and complex foundation drainage systems. We've stayed true to our roots: providing honest advice, keeping clean workspaces, and never charging hidden fees.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

At GTZ Plumbing, these four pillars guide every single diagnostic call and repair project we take on.

1. Absolute Dependability

We respect your time. When we schedule a dispatch time on Saturday or Sunday, we show up on time. If a delay occurs, we communicate instantly.

2. Master Craftsmanship

No shortcuts, no temporary band-aids. We evaluate the core cause of leaks or drain clogs, installing replacement fixtures and pipes to compliance standards.

3. Fair Upfront Estimates

We diagnose the issue, explain the solution, and write a precise cost estimate before turning a single wrench. The price we quote is the price you pay.

4. Local Relationships

We don't prioritize quick sales. We aim to build long-term relationships of trust with Orange County property owners, ensuring safety for years to come.
